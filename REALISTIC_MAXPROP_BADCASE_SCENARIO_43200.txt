Realistic MaxProp-Unfavorable Scenario (43200s) - Settings + Rationale
====================================================================

Goal
----
- Make a scenario that still looks "deployable in reality", but exposes MaxProp weaknesses:
  - non-stationary traffic (phase changes + spikes)
  - heterogeneous links (range/capacity changes + contention)
  - heterogeneous energy budgets (nodes die / duty cycle)
  - many short contacts (fast movers) + short TTL traffic
  - time-varying graph (backhaul appears only after incident)

Settings Files (same scenario, different routing)
-------------------------------------------------
- realistic_event_43200_maxprop_settings.txt
- realistic_event_43200_prophet_settings.txt
- realistic_event_43200_spraywait_settings.txt

Run Commands
------------
- Compile once:
  ./compile.sh

- Run (batch, 1 run):
  ./one.sh -b 1 realistic_event_43200_maxprop_settings.txt
  ./one.sh -b 1 realistic_event_43200_prophet_settings.txt
  ./one.sh -b 1 realistic_event_43200_spraywait_settings.txt

Outputs are written under:
- reports/

Core Scenario Timeline
----------------------
- Simulation time: 0 .. 43200 seconds
- Phase A (0..14400): background chatter (citizens/vehicles)
- Phase B (14400..18000): incident spike (large media uploads + frequent status)
- Phase C (18000..43200): recovery with periodic "aftershock" spikes + continued backhaul

Host Groups (total 124 nodes; node count is constant, positions change due to mobility)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Group 1 (citizens, smartphones): 80 nodes, ShortestPathMapBasedMovement, BLE-like link
  - buffer: 10M, speed: 0.5..1.8 m/s
  - energy: 15000..25000 (default Group.*)

- Group 2 (vehicles): 20 nodes, Wi-Fi Direct link (InterferenceLimitedInterface)
  - buffer: 30M, speed: 4..18
  - energy lower: 5000..9000, scan every 2s (more aggressive discovery)

- Group 3 (responders): 8 nodes, BLE + Wi-Fi, short TTL
  - buffer: 50M, speed: 6..22, msgTtl: 60s
  - energy: 20000..30000, scan every 1s

- Group 4 (drones): 6 nodes, DistanceCapacityInterface (capacity drops with distance)
  - buffer: 20M, speed: 25..60, msgTtl: 15s
  - duty cycling via activeTimes (periods of being "off")
  - energy small: 2500..4500 (fast drain)

- Group 5 (shelters / field hubs): 4 nodes, GridLocation (stationary), large buffer
  - buffer: 200M, wifi, "infinite" energy (1e9) and scanInterval=0
  - located within default world bounds (MovementModel.worldSize = 4500x3400)

- Group 6 (backhaul buses): 6 nodes, MapRouteMovement (data/tram4.wkt)
  - buffer: 200M, BLE + Wi-Fi
  - active only after incident starts: activeTimes = 14400..43200

Interfaces (to stress MaxProp with link heterogeneity)
------------------------------------------------------
- ble: SimpleBroadcastInterface, 200k, range 10
- wifi: InterferenceLimitedInterface, 12M, range 120 (contention reduces effective rate)
- droneWifi: DistanceCapacityInterface, range 250
  - transmitSpeeds = 12M, 6M, 2M, 500k, 80k (distance buckets)

Traffic (12 generators; realistic-ish but MaxProp-unfavorable)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Background (Phase A): interval 30..60s, 5k..40k, hosts 0..100
- Incident window (Phase B):
  - citizens -> shelters: 200k..2M at 10..30s (below phone buffers but still heavy)
  - responders -> shelters: 2k..20k at 2..4s (very frequent)
  - drones -> shelters: 2k..15k at 3..6s (short TTL + short contacts)
- Aftershock spikes (short bursts): 20k..200k at 2..6s, targeted to shelters
- Post-incident low-rate: 5k..60k at 120..240s
- Backhaul: shelters -> vehicles 50k..500k at 10..30s (keeps the graph time-varying)
- Responder coordination + drone->responder quick tasks + vehicle->shelter crowd updates

Why This Hurts MaxProp (intuition)
---------------------------------
- MaxProp's meeting-probability + transitive exchange assumes "recent past predicts near future".
  - Here, the graph changes abruptly at t=14400 (incident + backhaul activation),
    and drones/backhaul have duty cycles.
- Dijkstra cost based on P(u,v) can be misled when capacity/contact-time dominate success.
  - Short contacts + low BLE rate + bursts of large messages punish "probability-only" routing.
- Queue scheduling + drop use a threshold/hop + cost ordering; under high burstiness,
  the ordering can become systematically wrong for short-TTL or high-utility traffic.
- Energy drain changes who is even available for scanning/transmission; MaxProp doesn't
  model "future availability" explicitly.
